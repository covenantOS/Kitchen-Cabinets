South Tampa is where some of the city's most distinctive homes sit side by side, and Tampa Kitchen Cabinets provides custom kitchen cabinets, cabinet refacing, cabinet painting, cabinet installation, and full kitchen remodeling throughout the 33606, 33609, 33611, and 33629 ZIP codes. From the historic streets around Hyde Park Village and SoHo to the established elegance of Palma Ceia and the waterfront stretch along Bayshore Boulevard, we work on kitchens that range from century-old bungalows to brand-new luxury builds.

## A Neighborhood of Bungalows and New Builds

What sets South Tampa apart is the contrast in its housing stock. On one block you will find a carefully preserved 1920s bungalow with original details worth protecting, and a few doors down a recently rebuilt home with a fully modern footprint. That mix shapes the kind of cabinet work in demand here. Owners of historic homes often want cabinetry that respects the era, which means period-appropriate inset or shaker styling that looks like it belongs, paired with the storage and soft-close function people expect today.

At the higher end, and South Tampa skews toward higher home values, many projects are full remodels. Homeowners near Bayshore, SoHo, and Palma Ceia frequently want to open the kitchen to the living space, add a generous island, and invest in cabinetry built specifically for their home rather than pulled off a shelf.

### Custom Cabinetry and Full Remodels

This is the part of Tampa where [custom kitchen cabinets](/custom-kitchen-cabinets-tampa) shine. Designing cabinetry around a specific home lets us match the proportions and detailing of an older bungalow or create a clean, contemporary look for a new build, and either way the storage is planned around how the household actually cooks and entertains. When a project goes beyond cabinets, our [kitchen remodeling](/kitchen-remodeling-tampa) service ties the layout, island, and finishes together into a cohesive result.

Not every South Tampa kitchen needs a full custom build. Newer homes sometimes only want a refresh, and in those cases [cabinet refacing](/kitchen-cabinet-refacing-tampa) updates dated doors while keeping a sound layout intact. The right choice depends entirely on the house, and we are glad to walk you through it.
