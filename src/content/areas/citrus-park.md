Citrus Park is a comfortable, family-oriented stretch on Tampa's northwest side, and Tampa Kitchen Cabinets provides kitchen cabinet painting, cabinet refacing, custom cabinets, cabinet installation, and full kitchen remodeling throughout the 33625 ZIP code. From the shops at Citrus Park Town Center to the neighborhoods around Logan Gate and the trailheads of the Upper Tampa Bay Trail, we help homeowners refresh kitchens that were practical and well built but now feel dated.

## Citrus Park Homes and Their Kitchens

Much of Citrus Park is made up of stucco homes built with builder-grade kitchens, the kind that came standard with oak or maple cabinetry, raised-panel doors, and warm wood tones. These kitchens were designed to be functional and budget-friendly, and most of them still have perfectly sound cabinet boxes underneath a finish that simply looks behind the times. That combination, good structure with a dated appearance, is precisely what makes painting and refacing such a good fit here.

Because the housing stock is fairly consistent, homeowners in Citrus Park tend to ask for the same kinds of updates. A clean white or soft neutral repaint instantly brightens a kitchen and makes the whole room feel larger, while a door-style change through refacing addresses the cabinets that look most obviously dated. Both deliver a major visual upgrade without the cost or disruption of replacing everything.

### Cabinet Work That Suits Citrus Park

The most requested project in Citrus Park is [kitchen cabinet painting](/kitchen-cabinet-painting-tampa). Spraying builder-grade cabinets in a durable enamel gives a stucco-home kitchen a smooth, updated finish for a fraction of the cost of new cabinetry, and the result reads as fresh cabinetry rather than a paint job. When the door style is the real problem, [cabinet refacing](/kitchen-cabinet-refacing-tampa) keeps your boxes and layout while replacing the doors and drawer fronts with a current style and matching veneer.

For homeowners who want a bigger change, [custom kitchen cabinets](/custom-kitchen-cabinets-tampa) let us rework storage and proportions and, in many Citrus Park floor plans, open the kitchen toward the living space for a more connected feel. We size the approach to the home, so a project can be as simple as a repaint or as involved as a custom rebuild.
