## How New Tampa Was Built and What That Means for Kitchens

Most of New Tampa took shape from the 1990s through the 2010s, a long window that produced a consistent kind of housing stock: spacious master-planned homes with builder maple cabinetry, raised-panel doors, and the warm wood tones that were standard at the time. Those cabinets were generally built with solid boxes and decent hardware, which is exactly what makes them strong candidates for painting or refacing rather than tear-out replacement.

Because so many of these homes share a similar footprint, we see clear patterns in what homeowners want. The honey or medium-toned maple that came with the house often reads as dated now, and a fresh painted finish or a new door style instantly brings the kitchen current. Many of these floor plans also have the open space to support a larger island, which is one of the most requested upgrades in the area.

### Common Cabinet Work Around Tampa Palms and Hunter's Green

The most popular project we do in New Tampa is [kitchen cabinet painting](/kitchen-cabinet-painting-tampa). Spraying builder maple in a durable white or soft neutral enamel transforms a 2000s kitchen for a fraction of the cost of new cabinetry, and the smooth sprayed finish looks like new cabinetry rather than a paint job. When the door style itself is the issue, [cabinet refacing](/kitchen-cabinet-refacing-tampa) is the next step: we keep your boxes and layout, replace the doors and drawer fronts, and apply matching veneer so the whole kitchen looks brand new.

For homeowners ready to do more, [custom kitchen cabinets](/custom-kitchen-cabinets-tampa) let us add or enlarge an island, rework storage, and tailor the kitchen to how the household actually cooks. The generous footprints common in Tampa Palms and Cory Lake Isles give us room to design islands with seating, prep space, and storage that the original builder layout never included.
