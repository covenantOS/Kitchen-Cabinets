## How Semi-Custom Cabinets Give You a True Fit

Semi-custom is the smart middle that most Tampa homeowners are actually looking for. The cabinet boxes come pre-made in three-inch increments, from nine inches wide all the way up to thirty-six inches. We measure your kitchen down to the wall, the window, and the appliance opening, then choose the combination of sizes that fills your space cleanly. Where a run does not land on an exact increment, we close the gaps with filler and trim so the finished kitchen reads as fitted, not assembled from a shelf.

You drive the look. You pick the door style, the color, and the finish. For the door interiors you choose between a wood-finished interior look and a clean plain white, whichever you prefer when a door or drawer is open. Because the boxes are already made, you skip the long fabrication wait and the built-to-order markup, and you still walk away with a kitchen that fits your room and matches your taste.

## When Built-to-Order Custom Is the Right Call

Some homeowners want true built-to-order custom or solid-wood cabinetry, and we build that too. It is our premium upgrade, and it runs roughly two to three times the semi-custom price with a longer build timeline because every piece is fabricated specifically for your kitchen. If you have your heart set on solid wood or a one-of-a-kind layout that only built-to-order can deliver, we will design and build it to the same standard. For the large majority of kitchens, semi-custom hits the look, the fit, and the budget, and we will tell you honestly which path fits your goals.
